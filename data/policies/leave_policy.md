# Leave Policy

This policy governs all paid and unpaid leaves for full-time employees.

## 1. Overview

Full-time employees are entitled to three categories of paid leave: Sick Leave,
Casual Leave, and Earned Leave. Interns and contractors follow a separate
schedule covered under the Contractor Handbook.

## 2. Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of casual leave per calendar year, accrued
monthly. Casual leave may be taken in half-day or full-day blocks. Unused
casual leave does not carry forward and lapses on December 31 each year.

## 3. Sick Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement

Every full-time employee receives 12 days of sick leave per calendar year,
credited in full on January 1. Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the
following year and cannot be encashed.

### 3.2 Documentation

For any sick leave of three or more consecutive days, employees must submit
a medical certificate within five working days of returning to work. Failure
to do so may result in the leave being treated as loss of pay.

### 3.3 Extended medical leave

For illnesses requiring more than 10 consecutive sick-leave days, employees
should contact HR to transition into formal medical leave, which draws from
a separate pool.

## 4. Earned Leave

Earned Leave accrues at 1.75 days per completed month of service. A maximum
of 30 days of earned leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year.
Any balance above 30 days as of December 31 is forfeited.

Earned leave applications of five or more consecutive days must be submitted
to the reporting manager at least two weeks in advance.

## 5. Holidays

The company observes 12 national and regional holidays per calendar year.
The list is published annually in the first week of January.
