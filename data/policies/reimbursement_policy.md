# Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Internet reimbursement

All full-time employees are eligible for monthly internet reimbursement of up
to INR 1,500. Receipts must be uploaded via the HRMS portal by the 7th of the
following month. Claims submitted later than 60 days from the bill date will
not be processed.

## 2. Learning & development

Each employee has an annual L&D budget of INR 25,000. Approved uses include
books, online courses, certifications, and conference tickets. Travel for
conferences is reimbursed separately under the Travel Policy.

L&D claims require pre-approval from the reporting manager. Receipts must be
submitted within 30 days of the course or purchase.

## 3. Wellness

A monthly wellness allowance of INR 1,500 covers gym memberships, yoga classes,
meditation apps, or similar. Receipts are required; cash payments without
receipts are not reimbursable.

## 4. Travel

Business travel is reimbursed at actuals for flights (economy class), hotel
stays (standard room), and ground transport. Daily meal allowance is INR 1,500
for domestic travel and USD 60 for international travel.

Travel bookings should be made through the company's travel portal where
possible. Direct bookings require pre-approval.
