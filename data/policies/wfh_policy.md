# Work From Home Policy

## 1. Default schedule

The company operates on a hybrid model. Engineering, Product, and Design teams
are expected in the office three days a week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday).
Non-technical functions follow a four-days-in-office schedule, with Friday as
the optional WFH day.

Employees may work from home two days per week by default, provided their
reporting manager approves the schedule for the quarter.

## 2. Full remote arrangements

Full remote arrangements are approved on a case-by-case basis for roles that
can be performed asynchronously and only with written sign-off from the
department head and HR. These are reviewed every six months.

## 3. Equipment

The company provides a laptop and standard peripherals. One-time reimbursement
of up to INR 15,000 is available for a desk and chair for employees opting into
a long-term WFH arrangement, subject to manager approval.

## 4. Expectations while working remote

Employees working remotely are expected to maintain core overlap hours of
11am to 4pm IST, be reachable on company communication tools, and attend
scheduled meetings on video unless otherwise agreed.
